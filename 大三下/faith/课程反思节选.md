# Engineering logbook 课程反思节选

以下英文摘自车轩铭原有 engineering logbook 的两条普通课程记录，只选取 Conclusion 和 Action plan。英文原句保留；前面的中文提示是本次整理补充，不是原文。完整日志包含跨三年的其他经历，因此没有整本收录。

## Surfaces and Interfaces 模型适用条件

来源条目：Year 3-01，QXU5010 Surfaces and Interfaces。原条目记录的是小组课程报告；这段表达个人学习反思，不将组内实验写成个人独立完成。

这段的落点是：结果算出来以后，还需要说明模型为什么适用，异常结果需要什么证据才能解释。

### Conclusion

This report taught me that technical literature is valuable not only because it provides formulas and conclusions, but because it helps define the boundaries of a method. Mature engineering judgement should include why a model can be used, when it cannot be used, what may cause abnormal results, and what further evidence is needed.

### Action plan

In future reports, I will include an applicability check in the method section: model assumptions, experimental conditions and possible violations. I will keep and explain abnormal values instead of only pursuing neat results. If the conditions do not satisfy the original model, I will search for alternative models and explain why they are better suited.

## Composite Materials 制造缺陷与结论范围

来源条目：Year 3-02，QXU5030 Composite Materials，大三下课程实验。

这段把“以后更认真”写成了具体要记录的项目：尺寸、厚度、缺陷、断面，以及后续如何验证。它是学习反思，不能替代结构安全评估。

### Conclusion

This experiment made me more aware that composite testing is not only about measuring material properties, but also about learning to take responsibility for structural safety. Material selection, layup direction, manufacturing defects and testing method all affect the final judgement. For real structural applications, one successful test result is not enough for a strong conclusion.

### Action plan

In future experiments or designs, I will list key risks before starting. Test results should not only report maximum load, but also discuss sample dimensions, thickness, defects and fracture surface. For safety-related structures, I will avoid drawing overconfident conclusions from one test and will emphasise repeated testing and more standard validation.
